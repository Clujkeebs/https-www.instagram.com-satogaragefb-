What I make

Built to your specs, not a catalogue

Every deck is pressed to order. You pick the width, the concave and the wood — I do the gluing, cutting, sanding and finishing myself.

Custom decks

Your width, your mold, your wood. Pressed to order and shaped by hand — nothing gets cut from a batch of a hundred.

Wood and veneers

Standard maple plies, or an exotic veneer top and bottom if you want the grain to be the graphic.

Graphics and finishes

Natural clear coat, stains, solid colours or a custom graphic. Send a reference and we will work it out.

One-offs

Odd sizes, weird shapes, a deck for a gift. If it can be pressed, it is worth asking about.

Why here

Not mass produced

You are talking to the person who presses the deck. That is the whole pitch.

Factory decks come out of a mold by the thousand and get shipped halfway around the world. These get pressed a few at a time on a bench in my garage, and every one gets looked at properly before it goes in a box.

  • Priced before anything gets glued up
  • Photos while it is being made, not just at the end
  • An honest answer when an idea will not work in wood
  • Every deck sanded and checked by hand
How it works

From idea to in your hands

01

Send the specs

Fill out the custom order form — width, mold, wood and what you want it to look like. Not sure? Say so and I will help.

02

We sort the details

I get back to you with what is doable, the price and roughly how long it will take.

03

Pressed and shaped

Glued, pressed, cut, sanded and finished by hand. You get photos along the way.

04

Boxed and shipped

Wrapped up and sent out, with a tracking number once it is on its way.
