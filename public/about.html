About the shop

One shop. One standard.

No production line, no rotating cast of technicians. Just a set of hands that has been doing this long enough to know what lasts.

The story

It started in a single bay

What began as after-hours work on friends' vehicles turned into a full shop — because word travels fast when someone actually does the job properly.

The early days were nights and weekends: one lift, a borrowed welder, and whatever project had rolled in that week. The work was never advertised. People saw a finished build, asked who did it, and the next job booked itself.

That is still how it runs today. The shop has better tools and a longer waiting list, but the approach has not changed — take on work worth doing, do it properly, and let the results bring the next customer through the door.

How we work

The things we will not cut

Do it once

Shortcuts show up eventually — a rattle, a leak, a weld that cracks. If it needs doing twice to be right, it gets done twice before it leaves.

Say the hard thing

If a plan will not work, or the budget will not stretch to it, you hear that on day one rather than halfway through the build.

Your build, your call

Opinions come free and often. But it is your vehicle — the final decision on every detail sits with you.

Nothing hidden

Quotes are itemised, parts receipts are available, and the progress photos show the ugly stages too.

Take on less

Fewer vehicles in the shop at once means each one actually moves forward instead of sitting under a cover for a month.

Stand behind it

If something we fitted fails, bring it back. That conversation does not need to be a fight.

What comes through the doors

The list changes with whatever is booked in, but most of the work falls into a few familiar shapes.

  • Ground-up custom builds on a bare shell or frame
  • Restorations of vehicles that deserve another 20 years
  • Engine, drivetrain and performance upgrades
  • Suspension, ride height and stance work
  • One-off fabrication when the part simply does not exist
  • Straightforward servicing for regulars

Not sure which of those your project is? Send it over anyway — the form has a box for exactly that.
