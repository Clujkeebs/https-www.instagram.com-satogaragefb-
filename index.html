How it's made

Three days, start to finish

Pressed

Five plies of hard maple, glued and clamped in a hand-built mold. It sits overnight so the concave holds for good.

Shaped

Cut to the template, then the rails and kicks get sanded by hand until the shape feels right under a finger.

Finished

Sealed, graphic applied, taped up and rolled out. Each one gets checked before it goes in the box.

About

A one-dog operation

Sato Garage started as a mold, a clamp and a pile of maple offcuts. It's still the same setup — just more decks going out the door.

Everything is made in-house, so shapes and graphics change from batch to batch. If you want something specific, ask for it and it gets built.
